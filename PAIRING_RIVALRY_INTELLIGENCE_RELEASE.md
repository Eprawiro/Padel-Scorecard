# Pairing & Rivalry Intelligence

## Included

- Strongest positive partnership signals.
- Challenging partnerships.
- Toughest opponents and favorable matchups.
- Player-by-player relationship directory with search.
- Explicit evidence confidence: Early Signal (1 match), Limited (2), Medium (3–5), and High (6+).
- Directional pairing outlook with safeguards for negative chemistry and limited samples.

## Interpretation and safeguards

- Partner Chemistry Delta compares partnership performance with the player baseline.
- Rivalry Pressure describes historical difficulty only.
- A positive result with a small sample is not presented as conclusive.
- “Best Available Chemistry” may still be negative when no positive pairing has been recorded; in that case the UI explicitly withholds a positive recommendation.
- The module is read-only and does not modify Official Ranking, Official Rating, handicap, eligibility, match records, or tournament data.
